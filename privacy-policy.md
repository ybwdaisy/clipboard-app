# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026. This policy covers Clipboard for iOS/iPadOS and its keyboard, share, action and widget extensions. The maintenance changes described below apply to version 2.7.1; older installed versions may behave as noted separately.

## Clipboard content and permissions

Clipboard stores the text and images you choose to save, including through the share menu and supported clipboard access. iOS controls clipboard access and may ask for permission depending on your system version and settings. Optional background mode uses a visible Picture in Picture session; it is not an unrestricted, always-running system clipboard service.

History is stored locally in the app's shared container so its extensions can use it. The keyboard uses Full Access to read that shared history. Clipboard does not intentionally send saved text, images or typed content to the developer, an AI service or an advertising service.

## iCloud sync

When iCloud sync is enabled, saved history is synchronized through Apple's CloudKit service using your iCloud account. Content is therefore stored on your devices and in your iCloud private database, rather than only on one device. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to that service. Sync setting changes take effect after closing and reopening the app. Disabling sync does not itself delete existing local history or previously synchronized copies.

## Crash diagnostics

Clipboard uses Sentry, a third-party service, to help diagnose crashes. Diagnostics may include crash stack traces, exception type, app/build version, operating system and device information. We do not intentionally attach clipboard text, images, typed content, account information or advertising identifiers to these reports, and do not use them for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Version 2.7.1 disables performance tracing and profiling, screenshots, view hierarchy attachments and diagnostic breadcrumbs, and removes free-form exception details and extra fields before sending crash events. Older versions may still send performance/profiling diagnostics and may not have all these filtering safeguards. Updating the app applies the newer safeguards; changing this policy alone cannot change an installed older build.

Diagnostic reports are processed and retained according to the Sentry project configuration and service terms. See [Sentry's Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/) for information about that provider.

## Your choices

You can turn off background mode, change clipboard permissions in iOS settings, disable the custom keyboard or its Full Access, turn off iCloud sync, and delete history through the app. Removing the app deletes its local app data; it does not necessarily delete copies already synchronized to iCloud or stored on other devices. Contact us with privacy questions. The app does not maintain a separate user account with the developer.
